# Motion audit evidence checklist

Use [shared motion guidance](../better-ui/motion.md) as the source of principles and defaults. Inspect the categories relevant to the requested surface:

- Purpose and frequency: does motion aid the task without delaying feedback?
- Timing and continuity: does it preserve a useful spatial or state relationship?
- Interruption: do reversals and repeated inputs remain coherent?
- Performance: is avoidable work supported by a trace or other measurement?
- Accessibility: is reduced motion respected and state available without movement?
- Cohesion: does this surface follow the governing project system?

Source patterns identify candidates; confirm the effect before reporting a defect. Add opportunities only when requested or clearly relevant, label them optional, and never invent findings to fill a quota.
